Scoring & Tabulation Logic

Calculation Formula

{% if active_tab == 'overall' %} (Round 1 + Costume + Sports + Q&A) ÷ 4 {% elif active_tab == 'general' %} Total Points ÷ Active Judge Count {% else %} Total Combined Segment Points (Max 100) {% endif %}

{% if active_tab == 'overall' %} The Overall Final Score aggregates the averages of all 4 segments (each graded out of 100%). It sums their final scores and divides by 4 to scale the combined tabulation safely to a final 100% rating. {% elif active_tab == 'general' %} The Round 1 Score sums all 5 criteria (Max 100) and divides by the judges who submitted a General Show ballot. {% elif active_tab == 'costume' %} The Costume Score averages the sum of Creativity, Theme Relevance, Stage Presence, and Overall Impact (Max 100) from all submitted costume ballots. {% elif active_tab == 'sports' %} The Sports Attire Score averages the sum of Confidence/Bearing, Costume Style, Ramp Walk, and Overall Presentation (Max 100) from all submitted sports ballots. {% else %} The Q&A Score averages the sum of Intelligence/Content, Delivery/Wit, and Overall Poise (Max 100) from all submitted Q&A ballots. {% endif %}

Ranking Rules

  • Ranking is determined 100% by the final average of this specific segment (or overall aggregate).
  • The system automatically sorts candidates from highest average to lowest.
  • In the case of a tie, the HR panel may refer to individual scorecard metrics.
